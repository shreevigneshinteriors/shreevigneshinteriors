FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?

Here are clear answers to common inquiries.

What is the average timeline for a full home project?
Most standard 2BHK/3BHK interior projects take between 45 to 60 days from final 3D design approval to site handover.
Can I hire you for specific sectors (like only a Kitchen or Living Room)?
Yes! We handle both full home interior transformations and individual sector projects such as Modular Kitchens, Living Room TV units, or Master Bedroom suites.
Do you provide 3D designs before work begins?
Yes, we provide detailed 3D visual renders for all key sectors so you can visualize materials, lighting, and colors clearly before execution but it will be chargeable for 3D Designs.
What materials do you use for modular furniture and wardrobes?
We use high-grade BWR/BWP plywood, anti-termite acrylic/laminate finishes, and hardware from Premium Brands.
